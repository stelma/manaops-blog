# ManaOps brand resources

## Fonts

**Wordmark** — Georgia (or Times New Roman as fallback), serif, bold (700), letter-spacing 2px. Used for "ManaOps" only.

**Tagline / UI text** — Anthropic Sans (system sans-serif fallback), medium weight (500), letter-spacing 4–6px, sentence or upper case for taglines like "it's channeling."

## Shapes

- **Frame**: a peaked tower silhouette (triangular roof + rectangular walls), built from three nested layers — outer glow, dashed mid outline, solid cream-stroked inner edge.
- **Pedestal**: a widened hexagonal base flush with the tower's outer rim, holding three small teal dot accents.
- **Comet**: three tapered, overlapping flame-shaped tail layers (dark red → orange, largest to smallest) trailing a four-layer circular glow head, with two small fading dust particles beyond the tail tip.

## Icons

- **Sparkle**: a simple 4-point star/diamond flourish, used sparingly beside the wordmark. Teal (`#3FA08F`).
- **Comet mark**: the fireball-with-tail is the primary icon/symbol — usable on its own (without the tower frame) as a favicon or app icon at small sizes.
